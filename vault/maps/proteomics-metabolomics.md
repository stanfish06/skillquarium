# Proteomics & Metabolomics

> Mass-spec and affinity proteomics, metabolomics spectral analysis, and glycoengineering.

## Skills (10)

- [affinity-proteomics](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/affinity-proteomics.md) — Unified analysis pipeline for affinity-based proteomics platforms — Olink (PEA, NPX) and SomaLogic SomaScan (SOMAmer, RFU)
- [fragpipe-pyteomics-proteomics](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/fragpipe-pyteomics-proteomics.md) — DDA/DIA mass-spectrometry proteomics workflows with FragPipe, MSFragger, IonQuant, DIA-NN, Pyteomics, pyopenms, matchms, polars, and query
- [glycoengineering](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/glycoengineering.md) — Analyzes and engineers protein glycosylation by scanning canonical N-glycosylation sequons, describing S/T-rich regions, checking curated glycan evidence, and preparing NetNGlyc...
- [matchms](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/matchms.md) — Processes, cleans, compares, and searches tandem mass spectra with matchms
- [proteomics](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/proteomics.md) — Mass spectrometry proteomics QC, quantification, comparative analysis, and export for DDA, DIA, and protein-level result tables
- [proteomics-clock](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/proteomics-clock.md) — Compute organ-specific biological age from Olink proteomic data using Goeminne et al
- [proteomics-de](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/proteomics-de.md) — Differential expression analysis for label-free quantitative (LFQ) intensity data with standard MaxQuant and DIA-NN output
- [pyopenms](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/pyopenms.md) — Processes mass spectrometry data with pyOpenMS
- [sds-gel-review](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/sds-gel-review.md) — Review SDS-PAGE or protein purification gel images using DNA sequence, protein sequence, base-pair length, expected protein size, and lane labels
- [sec-report](../notes/proteomics-metabolomics/sec-report.md) — SEC (size-exclusion chromatography) analysis with peak detection, oligomer classification, and publication-quality PDF report generation via Typst templates
