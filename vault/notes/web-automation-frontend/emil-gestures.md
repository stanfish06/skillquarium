# emil-gestures

> [!info] What it does
> Build drag, swipe, and sheet interactions that track the finger 1:1 and stay interruptible. Use when building or fixing anything the user drags, swipes, flings, holds, or dismisses with a gesture — drawers, sheets, swipe-to-dismiss toasts and cards, drag-to-reorder, pull-to-refresh, hold-to-confirm — or when a gesture feels laggy, disconnected from the finger, jumps on release, or can't be caught mid-animation. Triggers on — gesture, drag, swipe, fling, sheet, snap points, Vaul, Motion drag props, rubber-band, pull to refresh, hold to delete, touch-action, "doesn't follow my finger", "jumps when I let go", "can't grab it mid-animation".
