# landing-page-design

> [!info] What it does
> Builds and reviews landing pages and single-offer marketing sites: intake questions, page structure, layout selection, conversion copywriting, SEO/AEO, and an opinionated visual system (Geist/Manrope type scale, spacing, radius, hero, motion, mandatory tagline section). Use when building, restyling, or writing copy for a landing page, a marketing or product-launch site, or a section of one. Not for dashboards, app UI, or projects with an existing design system; use frontend-design or design-taste-frontend there.
