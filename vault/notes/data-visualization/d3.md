# d3

> [!info] What it does
> Use when building, fixing or animating a chart with D3.js inside a React or Next.js app (barplot, scatterplot, line chart, treemap, map…). Covers the D3-for-math / React-for-rendering split, margins and axes, responsiveness with a useDimensions hook, animation with Motion, and switching from SVG to canvas for large datasets. Not for vanilla D3 projects without React.
